Gnome Garden Defense

Garden gnomes defend your cabbage from hungry slugs.

Controls

• Choose a gnome type then click/tap empty soil: place a gnome (cost varies by type)
• Standard is cheapest, splash and frost cost more, fire costs the most.
• Click / tap a gnome: upgrade it (cost 25 coins, stronger attack)
• Press S or tap Salt storm button: damage all slugs (20s cooldown)
• Press F or tap Fire storm button: heavy fire damage to all slugs (30s cooldown)

• Standard: fast single shots
• Splash: area damage around impact
• Frost: slows slugs for a short time
• Fire: burns slugs over time
• Anti-air: hits flying slugs only
• Sniper: high-damage long-range shots
• Machine gun: very fast firing rate

• Boss waves arrive later with heavy, slow slug champions.

Win: Survive all 30 waves.
Lose: 10 slugs reach the cabbage.
